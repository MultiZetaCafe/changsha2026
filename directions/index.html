注册地点和会议地点 / Conference Location

相关说明/Important Notes

本次研讨会不收取注册费，会务组将为参会人员提供免费餐饮，参会人员的住宿由会务组统一安排，住宿费用、交通费等费用均自理。
There is no registration fee for this conference. The organizing committee will provide complimentary meals for all participants. Accommodation will be arranged by the committee, but all lodging expenses, transportation costs, and other incidental fees shall be borne by the attendees themselves.

交通信息/Transportation Information

交通信息：地铁3号线阳光站1号口可达。美年21度国际酒店距离阳光站1号口约200米。
By metro: Take Line 3 to Yangguang Station and use Exit 1. Meinian 21° International Hotel is about 200 meters from Exit 1 of Yangguang Station.

公共交通/Public Transport: 长沙南站/Changsha South Railway Station, 长沙火车站/Changsha Railway Station, 长沙黄花国际机场/Changsha Huanghua International Airport （点击链接或向下滚动页面参考详细信息 / Click the links or scroll down for details）

打车参考：从长沙火车站或长沙南站至酒店，费用约30-50元；从长沙黄花国际机场至酒店，费用约60-100元。实际费用可能因路线、时段和交通情况有所变化。
Taxi reference: A taxi from Changsha Railway Station or Changsha South Railway Station to the hotel costs approximately CNY 30-50. A taxi from Changsha Huanghua International Airport to the hotel costs approximately CNY 60-100. Actual fares may vary depending on the route, time of day, and traffic conditions.

详细交通路线/Detailed Transportation Routes

一、从长沙南站（高铁站）出发

全程约56分钟 | 票价5元 | 步行约1.2公里

  1. 步行进站：从长沙南站步行约145米（3分钟）至长沙火车南站地铁站5号口。
  2. 乘坐4号线：乘坐地铁4号线（罐子岭方向），10站约22分钟。
  3. 换乘3号线：在阜埠河站下车，同站换乘3号线（湘潭北站方向），2站约5分钟。
  4. 到达目的地：在阳光站下车，出站后步行至酒店。
Option 1: From Changsha South Railway Station (High-Speed Rail Station)

Approximate travel time: 56 minutes | Fare: CNY 5 | Walking distance: approximately 1.2 km

  1. Walk to the metro: From Changsha South Railway Station, walk approximately 145 meters (3 minutes) to Exit 5 of Changsha South Railway Station Metro Station.
  2. Take Line 4: Take Metro Line 4 toward Guanziling for 10 stops, approximately 22 minutes.
  3. Transfer to Line 3: Get off at Fubuhe Station and transfer to Line 3 toward Xiangtan North Railway Station. Ride for 2 stops, approximately 5 minutes.
  4. Arrive at destination: Get off at Yangguang Station, exit the station, and walk to the hotel.
二、从长沙火车站出发

全程约42分钟 | 票价4元 | 步行约972米

  1. 乘坐3号线：在长沙火车站地铁站上车，乘坐地铁3号线（湘潭北站方向）。
  2. 下车：乘坐9站（约24分钟）后，在阳光地铁站1号口下车（列车左侧开门）。
  3. 步行至酒店：出站后步行约972米（约14分钟），或骑行约4分钟，即可到达美年21度国际酒店（中南大学阳光地铁站店）。
Option 2: From Changsha Railway Station

Approximate travel time: 42 minutes | Fare: CNY 4 | Walking distance: approximately 972 meters

  1. Take Line 3: Board at Changsha Railway Station Metro Station and take Metro Line 3 toward Xiangtan North Railway Station.
  2. Get off: After 9 stops, approximately 24 minutes, alight at Yangguang Metro Station - Exit 1. Doors open on the left side.
  3. Walk to the hotel: After exiting the station, walk approximately 972 meters, approximately 14 minutes, or cycle for about 4 minutes to reach Meinian 21° International Hotel (Central South University Yangguang Metro Station Branch).
三、从长沙黄花国际机场出发

全程约1小时23分钟 | 票价7元 | 步行约1.5公里

  1. 步行进站：从机场步行约246米（4分钟）至黄花机场T1T2地铁站2号口。
  2. 乘坐6号线：乘坐地铁6号线（谢家桥方向）。
  3. 换乘3号线：乘坐14站（约34分钟）到达朝阳村地铁站（列车左侧开门），站内步行约244米（4分钟）换乘3号线（湘潭北站方向）。
  4. 下车：乘坐8站（约21分钟）到达阳光地铁站1号口下车（列车左侧开门）。
  5. 步行至酒店：出站后步行约972米（约14分钟），或骑行约4分钟到达酒店。
Option 3: From Changsha Huanghua International Airport

Approximate travel time: 1 hour 23 minutes | Fare: CNY 7 | Walking distance: approximately 1.5 km

  1. Walk to the metro: From the airport, walk approximately 246 meters (4 minutes) to Exit 2 of Huanghua Airport T1T2 Metro Station.
  2. Take Line 6: Take Metro Line 6 toward Xiejiaqiao.
  3. Transfer to Line 3: Ride for 14 stops, approximately 34 minutes, to Chaoyangcun Metro Station. Doors open on the left side. Then walk approximately 244 meters, approximately 4 minutes, inside the station to transfer to Line 3 toward Xiangtan North Railway Station.
  4. Get off: Ride for 8 stops, approximately 21 minutes, and alight at Yangguang Metro Station - Exit 1. Doors open on the left side.
  5. Walk to the hotel: After exiting the station, walk approximately 972 meters, approximately 14 minutes, or cycle for about 4 minutes to reach the hotel.
